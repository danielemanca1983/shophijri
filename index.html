Meet the Founder

Where sacred scholarship meets the everyday Muslim heart.

“I didn’t want a faith that only came alive twice a year. I wanted a rhythm — something gentle, rooted, and tangible — that could hold the heart steady through all twelve months of the lunar calendar.”

My name is Masuma Abdul-Muhit. I spent over a decade studying and teaching the classical Islamic sciences (Aalimiyyah), and today I work as a life coach specialising in women’s wellbeing, success, and family life.

Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know about subscribing, delivery and how Hijri works.

Need help with an existing order or milestone?

Our small team is always happy to assist with subscriptions, address changes, or token rewards.

Contact Us (info@shophijri.com)
When will my volume arrive?

Pre-orders close on the 12th of each month, and your volume is dispatched within 7–12 working days after that date. UK orders are sent by Royal Mail 2nd Class, so allow up to 4 days from dispatch for it to reach your door.

Can I skip a month or cancel?

Yes. You can pause or cancel your subscription at any time through your account or by getting in touch with us. Just make the change before the 12th of the month so it takes effect before your next volume is prepared.

Is delivery really free?

Yes. Delivery is included in the price on every order, in the UK and internationally.

Can I buy a single volume?

Yes. Single volumes (£14.99) are available to order while the monthly pre-order window is open. Once the window closes on the 12th, that volume is not available for single purchase again, though limited archive copies of select earlier issues may occasionally appear in our 1448 Archive.

Do you offer refunds?

Because every volume is made to order, we don't offer refunds or exchanges and all sales are final. Your subscription itself can be paused or cancelled at any time for future volumes.

What is the Collector's Journey?

Every volume comes with a physical Collector's Token bearing that month's theme. As you collect tokens across the Hijri year, you move through three tiers — The Wayfarer (4 volumes), The Traveller (8 volumes), and The Companion (12 volumes) — unlocking exclusive milestone gifts along the way.

Frequently Asked Questions

When will my monthly volume arrive?
Subscriptions close on the 12th of each Gregorian month. All editions are dispatched simultaneously in protective custom packaging via tracked international postal services to ensure they reach your doorstep before the new lunar crescent appears.
How does the monthly subscription renewal work?
Your subscription automatically renews on the 1st of each calendar month at £11.99 GBP with free worldwide shipping. You will receive an email notification before each renewal.
Can I skip a month or cancel anytime?
Yes, absolutely. You can pause, skip an upcoming lunar volume, or cancel your subscription at any time with a single click inside your subscriber portal before the 12th of the month. There are no cancellation fees or hidden commitments.
Is international delivery truly included at no extra charge?
Yes. We believe access to intentional spiritual literature should have no geographic barriers. Worldwide tracked shipping is completely free for all subscribers across the UK, Europe, North America, the Middle East, and beyond.
How do I receive the Collector's Journey rewards?
Milestone rewards (the brass token display tray, archival slipcase, and gold-embossed annual anthology box) are automatically bundled and shipped alongside your 4th, 8th, and 12th volumes as long as your subscription remains active.
Can I purchase past volumes individually?
Previous volumes are occasionally released in very limited archival batches for one-off purchase at £14.99 GBP, subject to remaining warehouse stock. Active subscribers receive priority allocation for all archival releases.

09 Clarity & Reassurance

Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know about subscribing, delivery, and how Hijri works.

Need help with an existing order or milestone?

Our small team is always happy to assist with subscriptions, address changes, or token rewards.

Email info@shophijri.com
When will my volume arrive? +

Pre-orders close on the 12th of each month, and your volume is dispatched within 7–12 working days after that date. UK orders are sent by Royal Mail 2nd Class, so allow up to 4 days from dispatch for it to reach your door.

Can I skip a month or cancel? +

Yes. You can pause or cancel your subscription at any time through your account or by getting in touch with us. Just make the change before the 12th of the month so it takes effect before your next volume is prepared.

Is delivery really free? +

Yes. Delivery is included in the price on every order, in the UK and internationally.

Can I buy a single volume? +

Yes. Single volumes (£14.99) are available to order while the monthly pre-order window is open. Once the window closes on the 12th, that volume is not available for single purchase again, though limited archive copies of select earlier issues may occasionally appear in our 1448 Archive.

Do you offer refunds? +

Because every volume is made to order, we don’t offer refunds or exchanges and all sales are final. Your subscription itself can be paused or cancelled at any time for future volumes.

What is the Collector’s Journey? +

Every volume comes with a physical Collector’s Token bearing that month’s theme. As you collect tokens across the Hijri year, you move through three tiers — The Wayfarer (4 volumes), The Traveller (8 volumes), and The Companion (12 volumes) — unlocking exclusive milestone gifts along the way.
